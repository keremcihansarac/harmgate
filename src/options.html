Harmgate

Blocks harmful sites by default. Everything runs in your browser and no browsing data leaves it.

Harmgate is not running in incognito windows. To turn it on, open this extension's details in chrome://extensions and enable "Allow in Incognito".

Core filter

Always on. These categories cannot be turned off.

Your limits

Sites you choose to close, all the time or on a schedule. While a limit applies, the site stays closed. Adding a limit or making it stricter is instant. Removing or loosening one takes the same 5-minute wait, then typing a sentence by hand, so pick the schedule you want before you add it.

Allowed sites

    No sites allowed.

    Blocklists

    Harmgate checks once a day for a newer list. That request goes to GitHub and contains nothing about your browsing.
