Harmgate

Blocks harmful sites by default. Everything runs in your browser and no browsing data leaves it.

Harmgate is not running in incognito windows. To turn it on, open this extension's details in chrome://extensions and enable "Allow in Incognito".

Core filter

Turning a category off takes a 5-minute wait with this tab open and in front, then typing a sentence by hand. Turning it on is instant.

Allowed sites

    No sites allowed.

    Blocklists

    Harmgate checks once a day for a newer list. That request goes to GitHub and contains nothing about your browsing.
